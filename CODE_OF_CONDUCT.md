# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in this project should be respectful, considerate, and constructive.

Welcome people of every background and experience level. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to mesih.t@gmail.com, including relevant context. Reports will be handled with discretion and assessed fairly.
